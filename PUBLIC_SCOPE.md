# Public Scope

This repository is intentionally limited to material that is safe to share in a company review process.

## Safe To Share

- high-level problem framing
- system architecture and component boundaries
- technology stack at a general level
- retrieval and orchestration patterns
- Text2SQL-style design concepts
- evaluation approach
- production-readiness considerations such as monitoring, latency, cost, and reliability

## Intentionally Excluded

- company name
- plant names, area names, or internal system identifiers
- proprietary schemas, table names, prompts, or internal configuration
- real operational data, business KPIs, or confidential metrics
- source code copied from the enterprise environment
- credentials, endpoints, screenshots, or environment-specific deployment details

## Review Framing

This repository should be understood as a sanitized engineering sample, not as a full open-source release of the original thesis implementation.

The purpose is to demonstrate:

- engineering judgment
- AI system design
- production thinking
- communication quality

rather than to disclose internal assets from the original project environment.
